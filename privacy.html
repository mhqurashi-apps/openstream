Privacy Policy

OpenStream · last updated 15 November 2026

The short version. OpenStream is local-first. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no backend of ours. Your screen travels between your own devices, or through a relay you run yourself.

What stays on your device

  • Your Ed25519 identity keypair and the paired-computer list.
  • Connection settings, keychain entries and the pairing history.
  • Screen frames, cursor shapes and input events, for the lifetime of a session and not after it.

What leaves your device

  • Screen content. The host encodes video and audio and sends it to the paired client. The client renders it and sends input events back. Nothing is copied to a server of ours, because there is no server of ours.
  • Local network discovery. The host advertises itself over mDNS so a client on the same network can find it. That announcement stays on your local network.
  • Optional mesh and relay. If you turn on the embedded WireGuard mesh or point the app at a TURN server, traffic passes through infrastructure you operate — your own Headscale or NetBird server, your own Coturn, or a third-party relay you chose. Those operators see the connection, not the content.

What we do not do

  • No telemetry, no crash reporting, no usage counters, no advertising identifiers.
  • No account, no email address, no sign-in wall.
  • No sale or sharing of data — there is none to share.

Permissions the app asks for

  • Screen recording / screen capture — to send the desktop. On macOS this is Screen Recording and Accessibility; on Windows it is the desktop duplication API; on Linux it is the PipeWire or XDG desktop portal.
  • Input injection — to move the pointer and type on the host.
  • Camera — only to scan the pairing QR code, and only when you tap the scanner yourself.

Children

OpenStream is a general-purpose tool and collects no personal data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

Material changes are committed to the project repository with their date, so the history of this page is public.
