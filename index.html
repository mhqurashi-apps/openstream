OpenStream

Free & open-source remote desktop · in development

The short version. OpenStream is 100% free and open-source remote desktop. Your macOS, Windows or Linux computer streams to your iPhone, iPad or Android device. There is no account to create, no subscription, and no proprietary runtime — every dependency is MIT, BSD, Apache-2.0 or LGPL/GPL.

How it pairs

Trust is established with a one-time QR code or a six-word pairing code. An Ed25519 keypair is generated on the device and stored in the system keychain; there is no email, no password and no OAuth wall anywhere in the product.

Where the traffic goes

  • Local network first, over mDNS discovery.
  • Peer-to-peer media over WebRTC when a direct path exists.
  • An optional self-hosted mesh (Headscale or NetBird) and TURN relay, both of which you run yourself.

Current status

The first release is being built now, under a zero-defect gate: the Rust workspace and the Flutter client must compile clean, pass clippy with warnings denied, and pass their tests before a phase is called done. The cross-platform installers are produced by open-source CI on every push.
